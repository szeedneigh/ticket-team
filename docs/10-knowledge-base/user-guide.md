## Overview

The Knowledge Base is your central resource for finding answers to common IT questions and procedures at La Verdad Christian College. It contains comprehensive articles written by IT staff covering everything from password resets to email configuration.

**Key Features:**
- 🔍 **Powerful Search** - Keyword and AI-powered semantic search
- 📁 **Organized Categories** - Articles grouped by topic
- 🏷️ **Smart Tags** - Quick filtering by relevant tags
- 👍 **Community Feedback** - Rate articles as helpful or not
- 🔗 **Related Articles** - Discover similar content
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** - Access on any device

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Knowledge Base

1. Navigate to **Knowledge Base** from the main dashboard
2. Or visit directly at `/kb` from any authenticated page
3. You'll see the KB browse page with all published articles

### What You'll See

The browse page displays:
- **Search bar** - Quick search across all articles
- **Category filters** - Browse by topic area
- **Article cards** - Preview of each article with:
  - Title and summary
  - Category and tags
  - Author and publish date
  - View count and helpfulness rating
  - "Read more" link

## Browsing Articles

### By Category

Articles are organized into categories such as:
- **Account Management** - Password resets, account issues
- **Email & Communication** - Email setup, Outlook issues
- **Network & Connectivity** - Wi-Fi, VPN, network problems
- **Software & Applications** - Office apps, specialized software
- **Hardware & Devices** - Printers, computers, peripherals
- **Security** - Antivirus, secure browsing, data protection

**To browse by category:**
1. Click on a category badge or filter
2. Articles matching that category will be displayed
3. URL updates to reflect your filter: `/kb?category=Account+Management`

### By Tags

Tags provide more granular filtering within categories.

**Common tags include:**
- `password`, `email`, `wifi`, `vpn`
- `outlook`, `office`, `teams`, `zoom`
- `printing`, `scanning`, `troubleshooting`

**To filter by tag:**
1. Click any tag on an article card
2. View all articles with that tag
3. Combine with category filters for precise results

### Sorting Options

Sort articles by:
- **Most Recent** - Latest published first (default)
- **Most Viewed** - Popular articles
- **Most Helpful** - Highest-rated by users

**To change sort order:**
1. Click the "Sort by" dropdown
2. Select your preferred option
3. Results update immediately

## Searching Articles

### Keyword Search

**Basic Search:**
1. Type your query in the search bar
2. Results update as you type (debounced)
3. Articles matching title, summary, or content appear

**Search Tips:**
- Use specific terms: "reset password" vs "password"
- Try different phrasings: "email not working" vs "can't send email"
- Check spelling for better results

**Example searches:**
- "How do I connect to Wi-Fi?"
- "Printer not responding"
- "Change my email password"
- "Install Microsoft Teams"

### Semantic Search (AI-Powered)

Semantic search understands the *meaning* of your query, not just keywords.

**When to use semantic search:**
- Natural language questions
- Complex or multi-part queries
- When you don't know exact keywords

**Example semantic searches:**
- "I can't access my email from home"
- "My computer keeps disconnecting from the network"
- "How do I share a file with someone outside the college?"

**How it works:**
1. Your query is converted to a mathematical representation (embedding)
2. Articles are ranked by semantic similarity
3. Most relevant articles appear first, even if they don't contain exact keywords

**Note:** Semantic search may take slightly longer than keyword search due to AI processing.

### Search Results

Results display:
- **Article title** - Clickable link to full article
- **Summary** - Brief description
- **Category & tags** - Context about the topic
- **Metadata** - Author, date, views, helpfulness rating
- **Similarity score** (semantic search) - How closely it matches your query

**Empty results:**
- If no articles match, try:
  - Simpler, more general terms
  - Different phrasing
  - Browsing by category instead

## Reading Articles

### Article Detail Page

When you click an article, you'll see:

**Header Section:**
- **Title** - Clear, descriptive heading
- **Category & subcategory** - Topic classification
- **Tags** - Relevant keywords
- **Metadata** - Author, published date, view count
- **Summary** - Quick overview (if provided)

**Content Section:**
- **Rich text content** - Formatted article with:
  - Headings (h2, h3) for structure
  - Lists (bulleted, numbered)
  - Code blocks for technical commands
  - Images and screenshots
  - Links to related resources
- **Table of Contents** (desktop) - Navigate long articles
  - Sticky sidebar on right
  - Highlights current section as you scroll
  - Click any heading to jump to that section
- **Table of Contents** (mobile) - Collapsible accordion at top

**Related Articles:**
- Semantically similar articles appear at bottom
- Based on AI-powered content similarity
- Great for exploring related topics

**Feedback Section:**
- Rate the article as helpful or not helpful
- See overall helpfulness percentage
- Your vote updates optimistically (instant UI feedback)

### Navigation

**Breadcrumbs** (top of page):
- Knowledge Base → Category → Article Title
- Click any breadcrumb to navigate back

**Scroll Progress:**
- Table of Contents highlights current section
- Smooth scroll when clicking TOC links

## Providing Feedback

Your feedback helps improve the Knowledge Base!

### Voting on Articles

**To vote:**
1. Scroll to the "Was this helpful?" section
2. Click **Yes** (👍) or **No** (👎)
3. Your vote is recorded instantly
4. Vote updates optimistically (no page reload)

**Changing your vote:**
- Click the opposite button to change
- Click the same button again to remove your vote

**Why vote?**
- Helps staff identify useful vs unclear articles
- Guides authors on what to improve
- Surfaces best articles in search results
- Analytics show which topics need attention

**Privacy:**
- Votes are anonymous aggregates
- Staff can see overall helpfulness percentage
- Individual votes are not tracked to your account

## Tips & Best Practices

### Getting the Best Results

**Search Effectively:**
- ✅ Start with simple, specific terms
- ✅ Use semantic search for questions
- ✅ Try synonyms if first search doesn't work
- ❌ Avoid very long queries
- ❌ Don't use full sentences in keyword search

**Browse Efficiently:**
- Start with category for broad topics
- Use tags to narrow down within a category
- Sort by "Most Helpful" for trusted solutions
- Check related articles for additional context

**Read Thoroughly:**
- Use Table of Contents to navigate long articles
- Follow step-by-step instructions carefully
- Note any prerequisites or requirements
- Check the published date for currency

**Provide Feedback:**
- Vote on articles you read
- "Helpful" = solved your problem
- "Not helpful" = unclear, outdated, or didn't solve issue
- Your feedback improves the KB for everyone

### Mobile Usage

**Optimized for mobile devices:**
- Responsive design works on phones/tablets
- TOC collapses into accordion on small screens
- Touch-friendly buttons and links
- Search works identically to desktop

**Tips for mobile:**
- Use semantic search for natural questions
- Tap category badges to filter
- Expand TOC to jump to sections
- Rotate to landscape for easier reading

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**❓ "I can't find what I'm looking for"**

**Solutions:**
1. Try different search terms or phrasing
2. Browse by category instead of searching
3. Use semantic search with a question
4. Check related articles on similar topics
5. If still not found, create a ticket for IT staff

---

**❓ "Search is not working or showing no results"**

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Try a simpler search query
4. Browse by category as alternative
5. Clear browser cache if persisting

---

**❓ "Article is outdated or incorrect"**

**Solutions:**
1. Check the published date
2. Vote "Not helpful" to flag for review
3. Create a ticket noting the issue
4. Staff will update or archive outdated content

---

**❓ "Vote button not working"**

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure you're signed in
2. Refresh the page
3. Try clicking again
4. Check browser console for errors
5. Report to IT if persisting

---

**❓ "Images or formatting not displaying correctly"**

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Ensure JavaScript is enabled
5. Report the specific article to IT staff

---

**❓ "Table of Contents not appearing"**

**Solutions:**
1. TOC only appears if article has headings (h2, h3)
2. On mobile, it's collapsible at the top
3. On desktop, it's a sticky sidebar on the right
4. Refresh if it should be there but isn't

## Core Ticketing Engine
- Ticket creation with attachments
- Centralized dashboard for MIS staff
- Status and priority tracking
- Real-time commenting for clear communication
- Complete, unalterable history log for transparent audit trail

## Communication and User Engagement
- Real-time notifications for ticket updates
- Feedback and rating mechanism upon resolution

## Analytics and Reporting
- KPIs: ticket volume, average resolution times by category, user satisfaction scores
- Dashboard for at-a-glance insights

## AI-Powered Support Funnel
- Conversational AI Chatbot for first-line support (RAG-grounded)
- AI Assistant suggests relevant articles during submission
- AI-assisted knowledge growth: synthesize resolved tickets into draft KB articles
